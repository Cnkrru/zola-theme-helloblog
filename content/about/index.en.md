### On AI-assisted Programming

- I do not dislike AI-assisted programming, but I am not a fan of going all in on AI
- From my own experience, how much AI can help depends on your own knowledge and coding skills
- Whether it is frontend, backend or hardware, AI does make things much easier, but the code it writes still feels like a mess
- Enough said: AI is a good thing, but the result still depends on the person using it
